Pre-registered Hypothesis outcomes (Gemini)

H1Evidence-first improves groundedness.refuted as stated

Direct has the highest support rate (0.850 vs 0.821) — but only because it barely commits; evidence-first surfaces 2.25× more claims at near-equal fidelity.

H2Uncertainty-first reduces overconfidence.supported

Lowest ECE (0.641) and mean confidence — though all prompts remain badly overconfident.

H3CoT / differential read better without being more faithful.supported

Chain-of-thought: coherence +0.248, faithfulness −0.229 (both p<0.0001, paired Wilcoxon vs direct). Evidence-first +0.131 / −0.121. An independent rule-based claim adjudicator agrees in direction.

H4Prompt effects are larger on ambiguous cases.supported

Prompt-effect spread largest on rare (0.193) and hard-multi (0.158) strata, smallest on easy-abnormal (0.046).

Reading the whole record. No single number carries the story. A prompt that lifts F1 may be over-calling (Gemini, Tables 3 & 4); the same edit can be genuine on another model (MedGemma). Always read F1 against precision and accuracy, at a stated granularity, with the reference's own reliability in view (Table 6) — and the audit that produced these numbers is model-specific.
